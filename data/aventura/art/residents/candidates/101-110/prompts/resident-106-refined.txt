Character design:
A wiry older adult female duende with lightly tanned freckled skin, angular nose, knowing mischievous squint, green-brown eyes and ONE thick silver braid reaching her waist. A dramatic asymmetric deep blue pointed cap with its tip leaning backwards, an ochre patch and little copper stitch repairs; ears always visible. Ochre short open patchwork coat over a moss-green waistcoat and ivory shirt, dark plum knee trousers, red-and-cream striped socks, soft worn brown boots. Thin confident silhouette, raised eyebrow and lively crooked grin, practical stylish forest adventurer rather than an evil witch. No wand, no staff, empty hands.
Design refinements required by the art director:
Distinct face: angular hawk-like nose and pointed chin, strong visible smile creases, fine small amused eyes and one raised arched eyebrow. Lively adult face, no cute little button nose. A single acorn-shell pendant on a short cord, a small flat indigo satchel and one pink seed bead threaded into the silver braid. Her pointed hat is deep blue with a wide lopsided folded brim and a long sharp backwards tip, NOT another short floppy stocking cap.
EAR ANATOMY IS CRITICAL: each side of the head has ONLY ONE ear, with exactly ONE tip and ONE uninterrupted outside contour. Its little inner fold stays completely INSIDE the ear outline. NO second triangular skin spike behind or above an ear, NO double ear tips, NO duplicated ears peeking between hair and hat. In a true side profile or back diagonal, draw only the near ear; the far ear is hidden by the head. One ear on each side is visible from front and directly behind. Every hat brim has a fabric hem, never a skin-coloured triangle. Crisp uncluttered face silhouettes.
