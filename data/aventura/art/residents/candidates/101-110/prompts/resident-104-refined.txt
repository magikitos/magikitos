Style: exact same carefully hand-drawn matte storybook game sprite feel as the reference. Crisp small dark outlines, textured woven fabric, clear faces and pointed ears, delicious patchwork clothing and stitching. Natural human skin tones. Character design:
A plump adult female duende with warm golden tan skin, abundant freckles, round cheeks, dark hazel eyes, broad delighted smile and two small chestnut hair buns near her ears. A LONG soft coral-orange pointed cap drooping sideways with plum and moss fabric patches and a tiny dried marigold pinned to its brim. Plum-purple work dress with oatmeal blouse sleeves, irregular sage-green apron made of sewn leaf-shaped fabric pieces, striped cream-and-ochre stockings, short dark ankle boots. Playful sociable personality; comfortably round body, not a thin recolour of another girl. Little cloth pockets, visible stitches, no tools held.
Design refinements required by the art director:
Her face is pear-shaped and middle-aged, with a soft double chin, rounded short upturned nose, small laughing crescent eyes, crow's-feet and dimples, not the same large-eyed young girl's face. Personal accessories: a short necklace with one wooden strawberry-shaped pendant, a soft little side bag made from two stitched fabric leaves, and two contrasting blue cloth beads tied to the long cap tip. Keep the small marigold brooch. Warm coral hat against cool plum dress.
EAR ANATOMY IS CRITICAL: each side of the head has ONLY ONE ear, with exactly ONE tip and ONE uninterrupted outside contour. Its little inner fold stays completely INSIDE the ear outline. NO second triangular skin spike behind or above an ear, NO double ear tips, NO duplicated ears peeking between hair and hat. Every hat brim has a fabric hem, never a skin-coloured triangle. Crisp uncluttered face silhouettes.
