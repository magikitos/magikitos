Scene/backdrop: genuinely transparent alpha, no checkerboard, no background scenery, no ground, no contact shadows.
Style: exact same carefully hand-drawn matte storybook game sprite feel as the reference. Crisp small dark outlines, textured woven fabric, clear faces and pointed ears, delicious patchwork clothing and stitching. Natural human skin tones. Small adult woodland duende proportions, about 3 heads tall INCLUDING the pointed hat. High-definition original master suitable for reduction to 2x game textures; pixel art does NOT mean blurry or oversized artificial mosaic pixels. NO glossy/plastic 3D render, inflated shiny cheeks, porcelain toy, vinyl highlights, anime redesign or photorealism.
Layout: landscape 2:1 image, EXACTLY EIGHT equally wide columns and FOUR equally tall rows, 32 fully separated full-body sprites. Columns from left to right: FRONT/down, front-right/down-right, RIGHT profile, back-right/up-right, BACK/up, back-left/up-left, LEFT profile, front-left/down-left. Actual diagonal views. Constant gentle overhead three-quarter RPG camera, soft upper-left light.
Rows: first row neutral standing; second row walking with left leg forward and opposite arm forward; third row passing step with feet close; fourth row walking with RIGHT leg forward and opposite arm forward. Identity invariant: exactly the same face, body build, hat, hair, clothes, patches, colour palette and scale across all 32 poses. Turn asymmetrical details spatially; do not swap the character's clothes between views. Both arms, legs and ears anatomically coherent. Hands empty and visible where perspective allows.
Registration: centre each sprite in its own equal cell, feet on the same relative baseline; whole hat, ears and boots fully contained, generous transparent padding on ALL sides, no cropped silhouettes and no touching neighbouring cells. Do not draw grid lines, any text, names, numbers, labels, logos or watermark.
Character design:
An athletic young adult female duende with deep warm brown skin, broad luminous smile, expressive dark eyebrows and tightly cropped silver-white curls (not the previous dark curly hairstyle). Oversized turquoise pointed cap folding into a long soft hook backwards, rust-red cloth repairs along its band. A brick-red sleeveless patched waistcoat over an ivory rolled-sleeve blouse, charcoal roomy cropped trousers with one ochre knee patch, short tan boots, a mustard braided cloth belt and a tiny brass-coloured button at the collar. Bold resourceful adventurer, angular athletic silhouette, cheeky confident eyes. No glasses, no weapons.
Design refinements required by the art director:
FACE must be distinctly different from the former female style reference: elongated angular face, broad flat nose with a clear bridge, wide-set slightly narrow lively eyes (NOT huge round doll eyes), a strong chin and one eyebrow cocked higher; broad smiling mouth with a tiny front-tooth gap. Her little pale curls contrast strongly with warm dark skin. Personal accessories: a short necklace of three pale seeds, one diagonal cross-body strap to a little rust-coloured drawstring bag, no yellow sash. Use only one ochre trouser patch so the outfit is not symmetrical.
EAR ANATOMY IS CRITICAL: each side of the head has ONLY ONE ear, with exactly ONE tip and ONE uninterrupted outside contour. Its little inner fold stays completely INSIDE the ear outline. NO second triangular skin spike behind or above an ear, NO double ear tips, NO duplicated ears peeking between hair and hat. In a true side profile or back diagonal, draw only the near ear; the far ear is hidden by the head. One ear on each side is visible from front and directly behind. Every hat brim has a fabric hem, never a skin-coloured triangle. Crisp uncluttered face silhouettes.
