Character design:
A lean middle-aged female duende with warm olive skin, long dark eyes, strong brow, angular cheekbones and black hair in a short messy ponytail emerging below her cap. A small sharply pointed rusty-cinnamon cap tilted rakishly, cream stitches and tiny teal leaf sewn on the brim. Deep teal asymmetrically buttoned short tunic with rust elbow patches, ivory undershirt, warm cream cropped trousers with indigo knee patches, narrow dark brown boots, belt with a tiny fabric pouch. Outdoorsy roguish explorer with an amused lopsided smile, compact agile body, natural elegant proportions. No weapons, no giant cloak, no flowing floor-length fabric.
