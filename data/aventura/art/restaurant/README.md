# La mesa del bosque

Outdoor shared restaurant. Original kitchen shelter faces lower-right; dining tables,
lanterns, seats and food are separate existing forest props, not painted into the floor.

- Master: `sources/tile-kitchen.png`, generated with built-in image generation.
- Rebuild alpha/crop/definitions: `php scripts/prepare-restaurant.php`.
- Bake using the normal 2x integrated-reduction atlas pipeline. No runtime alpha scans.
- Original source is retained unchanged. Only transparent margins/keying are processed.
- Recipe text/audio lives in the website; the sprite grants no permissions or resources.

Prompt: production sprite, Magikitos cozy 2.5D forest. A reclaimed curved terracotta human
roof tile over irregular branch supports, moss and fern accents, tiny clay oven and preparation
counter. Open front facing lower-right, elevated orthographic three-quarter view. Warm matte
detailed pixel art matching the boot-house reference, clear small-scale silhouette, amber
lantern. Entire object, transparent background; no people, lettering, scenery or ground patch.
